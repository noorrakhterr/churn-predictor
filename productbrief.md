# Churn Risk Predictor — Product Brief

## Problem
B2B SaaS CSMs manage 30-100+ accounts and lack a systematic way to
prioritize which customers need proactive outreach. Reactive churn
discovery (e.g., learning during a renewal call) is too late.

## Target user
Mid-market CSM at a B2B SaaS company managing 40-60 accounts.

## Jobs-to-be-done
- "When I start my week, I want to know which 5 accounts need attention."
- "When I review an at-risk account, I want to understand WHY so I can tailor my outreach."
- "When I escalate to leadership, I need to justify the risk with data."

## Success metrics (if shipped)
- Leading: % of CSMs who check tool weekly
- Lagging: Reduction in surprise churn (churn not flagged 60+ days out)
- Counter-metric: False positive rate (don't waste CSM time)

## Non-goals
- Replacing CSM judgment
- Predicting expansion revenue (different model)
- Real-time alerts (v2)

## Why this matters
Acquiring a new B2B customer costs 5-25x more than retaining one.
A 5% retention improvement can increase profits 25-95% (Bain).
